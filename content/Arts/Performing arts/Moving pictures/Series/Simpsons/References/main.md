# Season 2 Episode 18 (Brush with Greatness)
- In the episode, [Marge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marge_Simpson "Marge Simpson") enrolls in an [art class](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Art_class "Art class") after [Lisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lisa_Simpson "Lisa Simpson") encourages her to revive her former interest in [painting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Painting "Painting"). When she wins first prize in a local art competition for a portrait of [Homer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homer_Simpson "Homer Simpson") on the couch in his underwear, [Mr. Burns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mr._Burns "Mr. Burns") commissions her to paint his portrait
