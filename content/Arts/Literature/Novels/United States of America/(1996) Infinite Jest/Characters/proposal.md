# Infinite Jest - Key Characters

## Incandenza Family

- [Hal Incandenza] [Combines tennis genius, linguistic brilliance, emptiness, and substance use] Hal is a prodigy tennis player and linguistic genius at Enfield Tennis Academy.
- [James O. Incandenza] [is the dead father, filmmaker, optics expert, ETA founder, and creator of the lethal Entertainment] James O. Incandenza is also called JOI or Himself.
- [Avril Incandenza] [is Hal's intense mother and ETA administrator] Avril is marked by rigid propriety, emotional strangeness, and possibly inappropriate relationships.
- [Mario Incandenza] [is physically disabled but emotionally perceptive] Mario is a gentle amateur filmmaker and one of the least broken Incandenzas.
- [Orin Incandenza] [is a former ETA tennis star who becomes an NFL punter and flees into sex and paranoia] Orin is Hal's oldest brother.

## Enfield Tennis Academy

- [Michael Pemulis] [is Hal's brilliant and streetwise friend] Pemulis combines math talent, recreational chemistry, and side dealing.
- [John Wayne] [is a Canadian tennis prodigy whose emotional distance makes him mysterious] John Wayne may also be involved with Avril.
- [Lyle] [is ETA's sweat-drinking spiritual advisor] Lyle resides in the weight room.
- [Schtitt] [treats tennis as a confrontation with the self rather than the opponent]

## Ennet House Recovery Center

- [Don Gately] [is a recovering addict and former burglar who becomes one of the novel's moral centers] Gately works at Ennet House.
- [Pat Montesian] [directs Ennet House with serious commitment to recovery]
- [Kate Gompert] [speaks honestly about depression and suicidal ideation] Kate is also a marijuana addict.
- [Tiny Ewell] [is a sarcastic and observant Ennet House resident]
- [Randy Lenz] [is an Ennet House resident whose psychosis turns violent]
- [Joelle Van Dyne] [connects addiction, beauty, filmmaking, MIT, radio, and the Incandenza family] Joelle is also Madame Psychosis and possibly the star of the Entertainment.

## Political Groups and Others

- [Remy Marathe] [is an A.F.R. member divided between separatist duty and personal loyalty]
- [M. Fortier] [leads the A.F.R. search for the master copy of the Entertainment]
- [Hugh Steeply] [is a U.S. intelligence operative undercover as Helen Steeply] Steeply tracks the lethal film's distribution.

# Incandenza Family

## Hal Incandenza

![Hal Incandenza](Images/image-7.png)

### Knowledge and Emptiness

- [MYTAKE] [Hal turns knowledge acquisition into a form of hedonism] I existentially connect with DFW through Hal because Hal's urge to accumulate total knowledge is seductive to me, but the book's revelation is that becoming Hal is not worth it.
- [Hal displays encyclopedic knowledge in the first chapter] His dictionary-like knowledge makes him seem autistic, hyper-rational, or unable to read what other people emotionally want from him. For me it was impactful, because in my first interaction with the book I saw something with similar levels of interest in knowledge.
- - [MYTAKE] [Doctor Jekyll (knowledge) vs Mr. Hyde (autism)] among human beings, this structure is ubiquitous.
- [Quote] [Hal performs the symptoms of grief rather than experiencing them] “A coisa toda foi de pesadelo. Eu simplesmente não conseguia sacar o que o cara queria. Eu fui lá e devorei a seção de luto da biblioteca da Copley Square. Não em disco. Livros mesmo. Eu li a Kübler-Ross, o Hinton. Gramei com o Kastenbaum e Kastenbaum. Eu li coisas como Sete escolhas: Dando os passos para uma vida nova depois de perder uma pessoa amada, 84 que eram 352 páginas de babação total. Eu voltei e exibi os sintomas clássicos da negação, barganha, raiva, mais denegação ainda, depressão. Eu listei as minhas sete escolhas clássicas e vacilei plausivelmente entre e em meio a elas. Eu ofereci dados etimológicos da palavra aceitação que voltavam no inglês até Wyclif e no limite até o francês languedocien do século XIV. E o terapeuta de luto nadinha"
- [Quote] [Hal's unforced-error statistics seem nearly unreal] "as suas estatisticas de erros nao forcados parecem um erro decimal"
- [Quote] [Hal defeats opponents so completely that they need oxygen] "Três adversários diferentes nos últimos meses tiveram que receber oxigênio nos tempos de descanso"
- - [MYTAKE] [I love this character symmetry, super man outside, lonely and depressive inside]
- [Tennis] [Hal is considered the fourth-best tennis player in the world at seventeen]
- [Addiction] [Hal's secret drug problem extends into compulsions around nicotine and sugar] Like many genetically hard-wired young addicts, Hal has severe compulsion issues around nicotine and sugar.

### Language and Postmodern Philosophy

- [Question] [Can total knowledge be reduced to formal language?] If knowledge can be formalized, then someone who memorized the whole English dictionary becomes a plausible candidate for knowing the limits of the world.
  - [Philosophy] [Wittgenstein makes language feel like a boundary of possible understanding] Wittgenstein said "The limits of my language mean the limits of my world", which suggests that understanding language's limits could mean understanding the limits of intelligible reality.
- [MYTAKE] [Hal as the perfection of the Enlightenment values while lacking what makes life worth living] The character is: rich, very high physical health, nation wide ranking at sports, extremely intelligent. But, he lacks ONE VERY IMPORTANT ASPECT, he is not loved. And in the end, that removes all the value of his other achievements.


### Family Pain

- [Hal's tennis matches against John Wayne, and its trauma] Playing tennis against someone who is having sex with your mother would create a strange mixture of anger, shame, and emotional confusion.

## Orin Incandenza

### Sex and Avoidance

- [Speculation] [Orin's sex addiction may be connected to an incest like pattern with Avril] After all his main target is 'young mothers of small children'
  - [Observation] [Avril's strained access to Orin highlights their damaged relationship] Avril has to call Orin's interviewer to find out anything about her own son.
- [Addiction] [Orin leaves his substance phase when he discovers sex]
- [MYTAKE] [Orin acts terribly because he runs from his inner problems] I am not sure he is simply a terrible person, but he behaves like one: he ignores Mario and did not even attend his father's funeral.

### Entertainment Plot

- [Theory] [Orin may have found the Infinite Jest master copy before everyone else] The novel hints that the missing master copy of Infinite Jest may have been hidden with James Incandenza’s buried head. By the time Hal and Gately seem to dig up the grave, it is already gone. Since neither the A.F.R. nor the O.U.S. appears to have secured it, one theory is that Orin reached it first and became the source of the circulating copies.
- [Theory] [Orin may give the Entertainment to the A.F.R.] Near the end, the A.F.R. captures Orin and tortures him with roaches. One theory is that Orin saves himself by telling them where the master copy of Infinite Jest is, or by giving them access to it. If so, the A.F.R. could use the film as a weapon against the U.S./O.N.A.N. government, helping provoke the political collapse hinted at by the novel’s later timeline.
