# Cosmic order

## Natural Order — The physical universe operates according to consistent laws.
Example: birth, death, seasons, gravity.

## Moral Order — Actions have ethical and spiritual consequences (karma).
Good actions tend toward flourishing; harmful actions tend toward suffering.

### Social Order — People have responsibilities (dharma) that allow society to function.
Parents raise children, teachers teach, judges uphold justice, etc.

## Spiritual Order — Souls are meant to realize their true nature and their relationship with Krishna. Attachment binds; wisdom and devotion liberate.
